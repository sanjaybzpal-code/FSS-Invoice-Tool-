How it works

  1. Upload your bank statement (Excel, CSV or PDF)
  2. FSS auto-detects the bank format and extracts credit entries
  3. Each credit is matched to a client by name in the narration
  4. TDS & GST are suggested based on pending invoices
  5. You review, adjust, and confirm — receipts are created automatically

Duplicate entries (same client + date + amount already in system) are pre-flagged and skipped by default.
